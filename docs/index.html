Digital Public Strategy Commons (DPSC)

Every plan to improve government technology rests on a theory of how change happens. Try yours: pick your agency, your goals, and your strategies, make a guess, and compare it with the model’s guess.

How it works: we describe a set of policy levers a government can use, combine them into every possible portfolio, and simulate each one many times based on assumed interactions between strategies.
